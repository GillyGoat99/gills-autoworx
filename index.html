About Us

Welcome to our garage in Annaduff, where experience, quality workmanship, and honest service come first.

Founded by Eoin Gill, a fully qualified mechanic with over 12 years of hands-on experience in the motor trade. Over the years, Eoin has built a strong reputation for reliability, attention to detail, and getting vehicles diagnosed and repaired properly the first time.

We specialise in BMW, Audi, VW, Skoda, and Seat, offering expert knowledge and experience with these makes while also welcoming all other vehicle brands.

Our mission is simple: deliver dealership-level expertise with honest pricing and friendly, local service. Whether it’s a routine service or a complex engine or electrical issue, your vehicle is in safe hands.
